A short history

Two rival pioneers, one merger, and a star that stood for travel on land, sea and air.

  • 1886Karl Benz patents the Motorwagen, widely regarded as the first practical automobile. Gottlieb Daimler builds his own motor carriage the same year.
  • 1901The Mercedes 35 HP arrives, named after the daughter of dealer Emil Jellinek, and sets the template for the modern car.
  • 1926Daimler and Benz & Cie. merge to form Daimler-Benz. The brand becomes Mercedes-Benz.
  • 1954The 300 SL “Gullwing” debuts with doors that open upward and a fuel-injected engine.
  • 1972The first car to carry the name S-Class launches, long the brand’s flagship sedan.
  • TodayMercedes-Benz is pushing into electric cars with the EQ range, while keeping its luxury and AMG performance lines.

Silver Arrows

Racing has been part of the brand since its earliest days.

1930s

Unpainted silver cars earn the nickname “Silver Arrows” and dominate Grand Prix racing.

1955

Juan Manuel Fangio wins the Formula 1 world title driving for Mercedes-Benz.

2014–2021

The Mercedes F1 team wins eight constructors’ championships in a row.
